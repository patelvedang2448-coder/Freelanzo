How Freelanzo works

Three steps from sign-up to your first conversation with a client.

  1. Create your profile

    Add your name, title and skills so clients know what you do.

  2. Showcase your work

    Add projects with short descriptions and images to build your portfolio.

  3. Connect with opportunities

    Clients find your profile and reach out to you directly.
